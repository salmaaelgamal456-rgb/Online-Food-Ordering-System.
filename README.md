# Online Food Ordering System

This repository contains the initial documentation and feasibility study for the Online Food Ordering System project.

---

## 1. System Request

### 1. Project Sponsor:
* Restaurant Management and Operations Team

### 2. Business Need:
* Expand market reach and attract new customers through digital ordering.
* Automate order processing and eliminate manual entry errors from phone calls.
* Increase daily sales volume and operational efficiency via a fast web and mobile platform.

### 3. Business Requirements:
* Interactive Online Menu: Categorized menu with descriptions, custom additions, and real-time item availability.
* Order Management System: Real-time order placement, status tracking (Received, Preparing, Out for Delivery), and kitchen notifications.
* Payment Gateway Integration: Support for secure online payments (Credit Cards, Digital Wallets) and Cash on Delivery.
* User Accounts & Profiles: Customer registration, saved delivery addresses, order history, and loyalty rewards.
* Admin Control Panel: Dashboard for updating menu prices, viewing daily sales analytics, and managing delivery routes.

### 4. Business Value:
* Tangible Value:
  - Expected 25% to 35% increase in total daily order volume.
  - Savings of 20+ hours weekly in manual staff time previously spent taking phone orders.
  - Projected annual financial savings and gain of $16,300+.
* Intangible Value:
  - Significantly higher customer satisfaction due to order accuracy and live order tracking.
  - Enhanced brand image and strong competitive edge in the local food market.
  - Complete elimination of order miscommunication and paper receipt waste.

### 5. Special Issues or Constraints:
* Must adhere to strict payment security standards (SSL encryption and PCI-DSS compliance).
* System interface must be fully responsive across mobile phones, tablets, and computers.
* Requires continuous high-uptime cloud hosting to handle peak ordering hours without lag.
